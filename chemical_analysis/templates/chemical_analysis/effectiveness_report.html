Management conclusion

The current analysis shows a {{ report.raw.improvement|default:"—" }}% raw SFOC difference during dosing, but only {{ report.normalized.improvement|default:"—" }}% after operating-condition matching. With {{ report.normalized.matched }} matched observations and {{ report.normalized.rejected }} rejected observations, the present dataset does not demonstrate a meaningful fuel-saving benefit attributable to the additive . The appropriate current decision is therefore NO DEMONSTRATED FUEL-SAVING BENEFIT — MORE DATA REQUIRED . This does not prove that the additive has zero effect; it means that a meaningful effect has not been demonstrated with the evidence available to date.
Recommended management action: The additive should not be continued on the basis of fuel savings alone at this stage. If there is a separate operational reason to continue the trial, continue it as a controlled data-collection exercise and collect additional dosing/non-dosing observations at comparable engine load, power, speed and voyage conditions. Once sufficient controlled observations are available, reassess the adjusted SFOC effect, statistical evidence and net financial case before making a permanent continue/stop decision.

Recommended next step: if the trial continues, collect additional controlled dosing/non-dosing observations at comparable engine loads, power, speed and voyage conditions. Then reassess the adjusted SFOC effect, statistical evidence and net financial case before making a permanent continue/stop decision.
